        Equation scope
        
                    
        S/N = (S G / Tsys) sqrt(npol t bandwidth) sqrt((P - W) / W), where G = efficiency x area / (2 kB).
        
                    
        This is the standard pulsed radiometer equation with the degradation factor fixed to 1. Enter the total on-source system temperature; SNRCalc does not separately add sky noise, elevation-dependent gain, scattering losses or spectral-index scaling.
