Volunteering Time

We appreciate everyone's time and effort in contributing to the ASTRAL Institute's non-profit programs. If you are someone who would like to support us, one great way is by volunteering some time and assisting mentors in running programs or public lectures. These programs aim to empower students in skills for the future, such as leadership, team collaboration and technical skills such as programming.

Financial Support

Everyone's contributions matter a lot to us, as our funding is used to organise and run programs with students and connect them with the people working in research institutions in Australia. This funding is also used to run outreach programs with the rest of the community through public lectures and other programs. We believe it is this community curiousity that will spark an interest amongst young people and give them exposure to such opportunities.

Our donation process is done through Swinburne University of Technology using the "Donate" link below. Note! To ensure that your support reaches us, please ensure the following:

1. Select the option "Other" for the field labelled "Please direct my support to"

2. In the "Other" text field, type "ASTRAL Institute"
